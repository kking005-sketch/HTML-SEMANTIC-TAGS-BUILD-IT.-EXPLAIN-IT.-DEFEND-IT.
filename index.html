Umbreon

Click to learn more about Umbreon

Umbreon is a Dark-type Pokémon introduced in Generation II. It evolves from Eevee when leveled up with high friendship during the night. Umbreon is known for its sleek black fur and glowing yellow rings, which help it blend into the darkness. It has high defensive stats and is often used in battles for its ability to withstand attacks.

Dragapult

Click to learn more about Dragapult

Dragapult is a Dragon/Ghost-type Pokémon introduced in Generation VIII. It evolves from Dreepy starting at level 50. Dragapult is known for its incredible speed and unique design, resembling a stealth bomber. It has a signature move called Dragon Darts, which allows it to launch its Dreepy offspring as projectiles in battle.
