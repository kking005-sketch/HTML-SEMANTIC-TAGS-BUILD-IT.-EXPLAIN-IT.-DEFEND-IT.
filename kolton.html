Garchomp

Click to learn more about Garchomp

Garchomp is a Dragon/Ground-type Pokémon introduced in Generation IV. It evolves from Gabite starting at level 48 and is the final form of Gible. Garchomp is known for its incredible speed and power, making it a formidable opponent in battles. It has a signature move called Dragon Rush, which deals significant damage to opponents.
